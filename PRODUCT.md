# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, no server. Profile and routines saved in the browser (localStorage). Deployable to GitHub Pages or as an Artifact.

## Users

Two audiences, equally weighted:
- Older adults (65+) who train on their own from a phone or tablet, at home or in a gym. Reduced vision, less dexterity, low tolerance for jargon.
- A relative or monitor who prepares routines for them; the older person then only runs them.

## Product Purpose

Make strength-and-mobility routines for older people trivial to create and follow. Success: someone opens it, gets a routine for the time they have, and finishes it guided by images, without help.

## Positioning

Routine built on the spot from two answers (time available, home or gym), plus a manual advanced mode; the run screen shows an animated photo and a one-line explanation per exercise, at a size an older adult can read at arm's length.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Quick creator: choose minutes and place (home/gym) → routine generated instantly.
- Advanced mode: pick exercises manually.
- Routines saved to a local profile; run any saved routine.
- Run mode: animated photo (two frames alternating) + short explanation, timer/reps, rest periods.
- Language: Spanish.

## Evidence on Hand

Exercise photos from free-exercise-db (yuhonas, Unlicense / public domain), two frames per exercise. No testimonials, no medical claims; must not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. One decision per screen.
2. Safety first: gentle exercises, chair support, clear rest.
3. Readable at arm's length: large type, large targets.
4. Nothing to sign up for.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum, body text ≥ 18px, touch targets ≥ 56px, respects reduced motion, high contrast, no time pressure without pause.
